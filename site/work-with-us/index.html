Part-time

We’re looking for a Marketing Director (Part-time)

We’re looking for an experienced, creative and highly self-directed Marketing Director to lead Voices Unveiled’s communications and brand strategy. This role will oversee our social media, digital campaigns, content creation, and visual identity — ensuring our message is clear, compelling, and grounded in the lived realities of Afghan women and girls.

You’ll be responsible for developing and executing marketing strategies that grow our reach, strengthen supporter engagement, and elevate the stories of our students and programs with integrity and care. The ideal candidate can work independently, manage multiple deadlines, and bring strong skills across social media management, copywriting, design, website management and campaign planning.

We’re ideally looking for someone with:

  • At least 3 years’ experience in a relevant marketing or communications role
  • Experience working in a nonprofit or mission-driven organisation
  • Strong skills in social media management, content planning, Wordpress and analytics
  • Experience with graphic design tools (e.g., Canva)
  • Excellent content writing, storytelling and brand voice development
  • Ability to work independently and manage multiple projects or deadlines
  • Familiarity with digital fundraising, email marketing (Mailchimp) and donor communications
  • Understanding of human rights, education, or international development contexts

How to apply: If you’d like to apply, please email your CV, a cover letter, and your portfolio or examples of your recent work (social media, design, campaigns or writing samples) to hello@voicesunveiled.org with the subject line “Marketing Director Application”.
