Program outcomes

Shaping futures, responding to needs.

  • Two full semesters of the flagship Self-Empowerment Course
  • Trauma-informed therapy for 5 students
  • Women's health sessions with doctors from France and the U.S.
  • 20 volunteers mobilized and 8 active mentorships sustained
  • Two women's arts programs sustained in Afghanistan
  • Life-saving relief for families: food, medicine, and financial support
