Volunteer

Share your skills. Help create a safe learning space.

Teachers, counselors, mentors, and organizers — there's a meaningful place for you on our team. We are a grassroots organization built on volunteer work and heart.

Get in touch

We are actively seeking

Volunteers to teach or lead sessions in:

  • Level 2 / B1 English
  • Entrepreneurship
  • Politics & International Studies
  • Learning and Organization Skills
  • Blog Editor

If one of these sounds like you, we'd love you to join our team. Please get in touch via hello@voicesunveiled.org.

Ways to volunteer with us.

Speak

Guest speaker

Share your expertise in our Self-Empowerment Course. We welcome inspiring professionals in mental health, women's health, holistic health, women's studies, the arts, entrepreneurship, or any topic that inspires you.

Teach

Workshop leader

Work with a small group of students once a week or twice a month in your area of expertise. We especially need conversational English tutors, English proficiency test instructors, Spanish, German and French tutors, creative writing coaches, and essay mentors.

Counsel

Support services

Offer trauma counseling, provide personal coaching sessions, or lead a support group for students carrying isolation, uncertainty, and loss.

Build

Specialized teams

Join our volunteer teams for marketing, fundraising, and administration. Have other skills to lend? We are open to your unique contributions.

Work with us

Current opportunities.

If you're passionate about gender equality, digital safety, and education, and you want your skills to directly support women in crisis, we'd love to hear from you.

Part-time

Marketing Director

An experienced, creative, and highly self-directed Marketing Director to lead our communications and brand strategy: social media, digital campaigns, content creation, and visual identity, ensuring our message is clear, compelling, and grounded in the lived realities of Afghan women and girls.

  • At least 3 years' experience in marketing or communications
  • Experience in a nonprofit or mission-driven organization
  • Social media management, content planning, and analytics
  • Design tools, content writing, and brand voice development
  • Digital fundraising, email marketing, and donor communications

To apply, email your CV, a cover letter, and examples of recent work to hello@voicesunveiled.org with the subject line “Marketing Director Application”.
