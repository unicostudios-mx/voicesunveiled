Student story

Laya, 20

Afghanistan
Story shared with consent. Some names and details may be changed to protect student safety.

“Real change begins within ourselves.”

I was a medical student before the Taliban came. From childhood, I had a special interest in medical sciences and always dreamed of becoming a doctor to serve the people of my country. Alongside my studies, I have a deep passion for reading novels, painting, and drawing; these forms of art have always been a source of mental peace and emotional expression for me. I am also interested in psychology and believe that understanding human emotions plays an important role in the medical profession.

I pursue learning English with strong motivation because I hope to one day connect with global scientific resources and expand my experiences internationally. Despite the unfortunate restrictions placed on girls’ education in my country, I continue to move forward in my learning journey with determination and hope. I see this course as an opportunity to enrich my knowledge and benefit from the perspectives and experiences of others.

One of the most important tools that I’ve always relied on alongside my studies to stay motivated and mentally balanced is painting. For me, this art is not just a hobby, it’s a way to express my inner emotions, better understand the world, and soothe my mind during challenging times. When I paint, I feel I can speak through colors without needing words. I especially enjoy painting natural landscapes, facial expressions, and abstract psychological themes, because I believe understanding human feelings is vital in both art and medicine.

In the future, I hope to use this art as a therapeutic tool alongside my medical profession, especially to support those who suffer from mental health challenges. One of my key strengths is having a strong internal motivation for learning and personal growth. Despite the difficult conditions in my country, I’ve never allowed hopelessness to stop my progress. I always try to find learning opportunities even in the heart of hardship.

I believe that real change begins within ourselves, and this inner drive is what helps me take steps each day with hope and effort toward my goals. Participating in courses like this one is not just an educational experience for me, it is a chance to strengthen my belief in my abilities, expand my perspective, and take meaningful steps toward a brighter future. My future goals focus both on personal growth and making a positive impact on society.

In the long term, I hope to work in a profession where I can use my skills to support others—whether in education, counseling, design, or social work. I want to be someone who makes a positive difference in people’s lives, especially those who face challenges similar to mine. Achieving these goals requires effort and dedication, and I am ready to walk this path with determination and an open mind. I don’t see education as just a personal opportunity—I see it as a way to serve my community.

Through this course, I hope to strengthen my professional skills and regain the motivation and self-confidence I need to pursue my academic and career goals. I see this course as an opportunity to recognize and enhance my abilities within an engaging learning environment. I also believe that participating in this course will inspire me and help improve my mental well-being -especially during a time when social pressures and limitations have affected my emotional state.

By learning new topics and interacting with others, I hope to find renewed energy and hope to move forward toward my goals.
