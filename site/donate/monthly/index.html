EMPOWER MORE WOMEN WITH US

Join the global community that has supported 250 students and their families since 2023

Thank you for being here. Your ongoing support fuels education, safety, and opportunity for Afghan women and girls—month after month. Reliable giving means we can act the moment help is needed.

Your Monthly Support Ensures Afghan Women and Girls Can:

  • Access free online education, systematically denied to them under Taliban restrictions.
  • Receive mental health support and a vital community, helping them cope with extreme isolation, violence, uncertainty, and trauma.
  • Receive urgent, life-saving support like food and medical aid, for themselves and their families.
  • Reclaim their voices and build skills through courses in self-empowerment, English, data analytics, philosophy, meditation and more.
