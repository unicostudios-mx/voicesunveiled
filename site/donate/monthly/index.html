Monthly giving

Become a Monthly Lifeline.

Thank you for showing up. By becoming a monthly donor, you give Afghan women and girls reliable access to education, safety, and opportunity — month after month.

Join the global community that has supported 250 students and their families since 2023. Your monthly gift keeps our Self-Empowerment and Feminism course and over 13 electives running. Reliable giving means we can plan ahead and act the moment help is needed.

A very generous gift of $300 a month sponsors a student for one trimester, covering learning materials, mentorship, and support, or provides food for a family for one month. Or choose your own monthly amount.

Give now

Prefer to give once? Switch to a one-time gift.

Voices Unveiled is a program of Café de la Culture, a 501(c)(3). Your gift is tax-deductible. You will complete your gift on our secure donation page.

Why monthly giving matters

Crisis doesn't wait for a fundraiser.

  • Fund more scholarships for women and girls ready to learn
  • Provide emergency aid the moment it's needed
  • Expand programs so no one is turned away

What you'll receive

Close to the work, all year.

  • Annual impact report showing exactly where your gift goes
  • A personal letter from a student
  • Invitations to virtual events with Afghan women in our programs
  • Confidence that your support continues, no matter what
