More ways to give

Tax-smart giving through your DAF or IRA.

Give through your donor-advised fund (DAF)

If you have a donor-advised fund, you can recommend a grant to us in minutes. Please direct your gift to our legal entity:

  • Café de la Culture
    Voices Unveiled is a program of Café de la Culture
    501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization · EIN 99-0810138

Search for “Café de la Culture” in your DAF portal; grants are routed by legal name and EIN, not by our program name.

Give from your IRA (Qualified Charitable Distribution)

If you are 70½ or older, you can give directly from your IRA to Café de la Culture. A qualified charitable distribution counts toward your required minimum distribution and is generally excluded from your taxable income — a tax-smart way to make a bigger impact. Ask your IRA custodian to send your gift directly to:

  • Café de la Culture
    270 North Ave., Suite 811, New Rochelle, NY 10801
    EIN 99-0810138

To count for this tax year, your gift must be completed by December 31. We're happy to help: hello@voicesunveiled.org.

This is general information, not tax advice; please check with your financial or tax advisor.

Your support ensures

Afghan women and girls can:

  • Access free online education, systematically denied to them under Taliban restrictions.
  • Receive mental health support and community connection, helping them cope with isolation, trauma, and fear.
  • Receive urgent, life-saving aid, including food, medicine, and financial assistance in emergencies.
  • Reclaim their voices through courses in self-empowerment, English, data analytics, philosophy, meditation, and more.

Questions

Giving with confidence.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Voices Unveiled is a program of Café de la Culture, a registered 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization (EIN 99-0810138). Your gift is tax-deductible to the full extent allowed by law.

Where does my money go?

Directly to scholarships, internet access, course materials, mental health support, and emergency assistance for Afghan women and girls. Our annual impact reports show exactly where every gift goes.

Can I give monthly?

Yes. Monthly Lifeline partners give a recurring gift of any amount, providing the steady support our students rely on and letting us act the moment help is needed.

Having trouble donating?

If you experience any issues while submitting your donation, please contact us at hello@voicesunveiled.org. You can also try clearing your browser cache, using a different browser, or an incognito window.

Thank you for your generous donation. Your support not only empowers the women of Afghanistan but also contributes to a global movement towards equality, education, and change. Every donation brings us closer to a world where every voice is heard and valued.
