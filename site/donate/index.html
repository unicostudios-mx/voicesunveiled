More ways to give

Give through your donor-advised fund (DAF)

If you have a donor-advised fund, you can recommend a grant to us in minutes. Please direct your gift to our legal entity:

  • Café de la Culture (Voices Unveiled is a program of Café de la Culture)
    501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization
    EIN: 99-0810138

Give from your IRA (Qualified Charitable Distribution)

If you are 70½ or older, you can give directly from your IRA to Café de la Culture. A qualified charitable distribution (QCD) counts toward your required minimum distribution and is generally excluded from your taxable income — a tax-smart way to make a bigger impact. Ask your IRA custodian to send your gift directly to:

  • Café de la Culture
    270 North Ave., Suite 811, New Rochelle, NY 10801
    EIN: 99-0810138

To count for this tax year, your gift must be completed by December 31. We're happy to help, contact us at hello@voicesunveiled.org.

This is general information, not tax advice; please check with your financial or tax advisor.
