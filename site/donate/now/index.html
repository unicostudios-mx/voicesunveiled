What We’ve Built Together

For almost three years, Voices Unveiled has empowered Afghan girls and women with:

  • Free online classes for these students, who are banned from school by the Taliban
  • Mental health support during exile, isolation, and trauma to 20+ families
  • 13 elective courses in tech, science, humanities, and arts
  • 6 rounds of our Self-Empowerment Programs that have helped them rebuild their confidence
  • Life-saving relief — food, medicine, financial support

250+ students have learned, healed, and reimagined their futures. 100% report that their lives have been transformed.

But without your urgent support, it could all disappear.

Your Support Ensures Afghan Women and Girls Can:

  • Access to free online education, systematically denied to them under Taliban restrictions.
  • Receive mental health support and community connection, helping them cope with isolation, trauma, and fear.
  • Receive urgent, life-saving aid — including food, medicine, and financial assistance in emergencies.
  • Reclaim their voices through courses in self-empowerment, English, data analytics, philosophy, meditation, and more.

Every dollar keeps hope alive. Your generosity today protects their education, their dignity, and their dreams.
