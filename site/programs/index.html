Programs

Education, healing, and leadership.

Free, online, and built around the whole person — from the classroom to mental health to career. Offered free of charge through Cafe de la Culture, our programs produce tangible improvements in self-expression, self-compassion, confidence, and optimism.

Education

Self-Empowerment Course

A free, three-month intensive course meeting three times a week. Mental health, women's health, leadership, critical thinking, and human rights.

 Mental health

Holistic & Integrative Education

Trauma-informed mental health support, academic guidance, and one-on-one sessions for personal, academic, and professional goals.

 Skills

Elective Courses & Mentorship

English, exam prep, data analytics, computer programming, philosophy of physics, creative writing, and more, taught by volunteer instructors worldwide.

 Access

Internet Funding

Internet packages so students can reliably join their classes from anywhere, safely and anonymously.

 Relief

Emergency Assistance

Food, medicine, and essential vitamins for students and their families facing critical hardship.

 Community

Distinguished Guest Speakers

Global leaders, practitioners, activists, and artists who open doors to new ideas in the Empowerment Course.
