Programs · Relief

Emergency assistance.

True self-empowerment encompasses self-care, proper nutrition, and health. We raise funds to meet the critical food and medical needs of our students and their families.

Give emergency aid

Surviving, and thriving, despite hardship.

Amid the ongoing crisis, many families are unable to provide adequate nourishment, leading to preventable health issues among our young women, including scurvy, rickets, and anemia. These conditions cause severe symptoms such as bleeding gums, bone and joint pain, chronic headaches, dizziness, fatigue, and profound depression.

Such health challenges make it incredibly difficult for students to focus on their education and achieve their goals. At Voices Unveiled, we take a holistic approach to support our students' success on every level. By contributing, you help ensure that our Afghan students not only survive but also thrive.

  • $100Buys a family of six groceries for one month
  • $200Pays for emergency medicine and health needs for one month
  • $400Pays for health, food, and essential vitamin needs for one month

No contribution is too big or small.

Health specialists

Are you a health practitioner? Volunteer remotely with us.

We invite health specialists and practitioners to share their knowledge and skills remotely with the women who need it most. Whether you're experienced in mental health, nutrition, general healthcare, or another field related to wellness, your expertise can help us provide essential health support, education, and resources.
