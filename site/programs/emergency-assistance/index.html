Emergency Assistance

We are committed to raising funds to meet the critical food and medical needs of our students. True self-empowerment encompasses self-care, proper nutrition, and health. Amid the ongoing crisis, many families are unable to provide adequate nourishment, leading to preventable health issues among our young women, including scurvy, rickets, and anemia. These conditions cause severe symptoms such as bleeding gums, bone and joint pain, chronic headaches, dizziness, fatigue, and profound depression.

Such health challenges make it incredibly difficult for students to focus on their education and achieve their goals. At Voices Unveiled, we take a holistic approach to support our students' success on every level. By contributing, you can help ensure that our Afghan students not only survive but also thrive despite these hardships. Join us in making a meaningful difference.

  • $100buys a family of 6 groceries for one month
  • $200pays for emergency medicine and health needs for one month
  • $400pays for health, food and essential vitamin needs for one month

No contribution is too big or small.

Are you a health specialist or practitioner? Volunteer remotely with us!

We invite health specialists and practitioners to join our mission and make a profound impact. By volunteering with us, you’ll have the opportunity to share your invaluable knowledge and skills remotely with individuals who need it most. Your expertise can help us provide essential health support, education, and resources to those facing critical challenges.

Whether you're experienced in mental health, nutrition, general healthcare, or any other field related to wellness, your contribution will be instrumental in enhancing the well-being of women in Afghanistan. Together, we can create a healthier future and empower individuals to have access to good information, overcome obstacles and lead fulfilling lives.

Join us and be a part of something transformative. Your involvement can make a significant difference in the lives of many. Reach out today and discover how you can help shape a better tomorrow.
