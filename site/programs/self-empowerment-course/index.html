Programs · Empowerment Course

The Self-Empowerment Course

Sponsor a student in Afghanistan, for a full 3 month intensive self-empowerment course. Help us build brighter futures - women are the key to making peace that lasts.

Sponsor a student

In a country where education is banned, self-expression is forbidden, self-empowerment is discouraged, and feminism is illegal, our course offers a lifeline. Students risk their lives to attend our underground online sessions, which meet three times a week. This reflects just how crucial and transformative this education is for them. They seek to learn about their rights, to express themselves freely, and to have their existence acknowledged. They are in urgent need of external support. Our course provides a comprehensive exploration of mental health practices, aiming to build self-compassion, self-esteem, self-awareness, and self-care. Each trimester features a diverse range of holistic topics, including creative writing, discourse, guest speakers, health and nutrition, mindfulness and women's studies.

Sponsor a Student

Our course extends across Afghanistan, reaching students aged 15-40 and offering them a vital connection to their own sense of humanity and relevance. Many have expressed that, since the U.S. withdrawal and the Taliban’s rise to power, this program has been a crucial lifeline, making them feel truly seen and heard for the first time in years.

Offered free of charge through Cafe de la Culture, our programs produce tangible improvements in knowledge of women’s studies, self-expression, self-compassion, confidence, and optimism.

Looking for other ways to help? Join us as a Volunteer Facilitator!

At Voices Unveiled, we believe in the power of shared knowledge and experience. Our mission to support and empower women and girls in Afghanistan, is enriched by the dedication and expertise of our volunteers.

We are constantly seeking passionate individuals to facilitate workshops or lectures for our students. Whether you have experience in mental health, women’s studies, nutrition, the arts, language, sciences or any other field that can contribute to their growth, we welcome your involvement.

Why Volunteer With Us?

  • Make a Tangible Impact: Your knowledge and skills can directly influence the lives of women striving for education and empowerment in challenging circumstances.
  • Engage in Meaningful Work: Facilitate workshops and lectures that offer valuable insights, practical skills, and inspiration to our students.
  • Grow and Learn: Share your expertise while also gaining new perspectives and experiences from engaging with our diverse student body.

If you’re interested in volunteering and would like to learn more about how you can contribute, please reach out to us by following the 'Contact Us' button below. We’ll be happy to provide more information on the available opportunities and discuss how your involvement can align with our program needs.

Together, we create an environment where knowledge flourishes, and every woman has the chance to thrive. Join us in making a meaningful difference—your expertise and passion are invaluable to our mission!
