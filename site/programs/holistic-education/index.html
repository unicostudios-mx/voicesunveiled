Programs · Holistic and Integrative Education

At Voices Unveiled, we believe that every woman and girl in Afghanistan deserves not just education, but the emotional strength, healing, and community support to truly thrive.

Our educational approach is trauma-informed and culturally sensitive, helping our students to feel understood, not judged. We aim to uplift spirits while strengthening academic skills.

Mental Health Support

We believe that education is not just about acquiring knowledge, it’s about healing, connecting, and reclaiming self-worth and creative expression. Many women and girls in Afghanistan continue to endure extreme isolation, violence, uncertainty and trauma due to Taliban restrictions and gender apartheid. Our mental health support meets this reality with empathy, purpose, and deep respect for each individual’s unique path forward.

Academic Support

We empower students to break through barriers to academic achievement, establish clear objectives, and maintain their drive to succeed. Beyond our courses, we connect students with broader educational pathways and ensure they receive comprehensive support for lasting success.

Elective Courses

In addition to our core empowerment curriculum, Voices Unveiled offers a variety of elective courses, taught by dedicated volunteer instructors from around the world. These courses are designed to develop skills, inspire creativity, and expand opportunities according to student interest and demand for academic and professional goals.

Functional Skills:

English Language (Levels 1, 2, and 3)

Structured lessons and test preparation for TOEFL and Duolingo, supporting students to improve their skills, and access global education and career opportunities.

Data Analytics

An introduction to analyzing and interpreting data using simple tools and real-life examples, preparing students for modern job markets.

Computer Programming

Foundational programming skills using beginner-friendly languages, with hands-on practice and mentorship from international tech volunteers.

Humanities:

Philosophy and Physics

A unique opportunity to explore deep questions about existence, logic, and the universe, learning scientific theories and sparking curiosity and critical thinking.

Creative Writing

Explores self-discovery and expression through journaling, storytelling, and poetry while strengthening creativity, writing skills, and community connection.

The Life of Ideas

A journey into how we think, why we believe what we do, and how ideas shape the world—and us. Students explore the big patterns of thought that run through philosophy and everyday life, from separation to unity, and from confusion to wisdom. This isn’t just a class about ideas—it’s about discovering how thinking itself can become alive, creative, and your own.

Art-Making - What Lives Within

A journey through the inner world through artistic expression, using drawing and other art forms to transform thoughts and emotions into visual stories that offer a path to healing, self-discovery, and empowerment.

Sciences:

Medical Science

Led by a practicing physician this class explores anatomy, neuroscience, cell biology, infectious disease, and more.

To the Stars and Beyond

Explore the wonders of astronomy — from the Big Bang to the galaxies above us. This class celebrates discovery, curiosity, and the contributions of women in science, helping students see their place in the vast universe.

Bio Science and Sustainability

Explore the exciting world of biology and ecology to better understand the world around us and create a more sustainable future.

Wellness:

Meditation

A supportive space for students to build inner peace and resilience through mindfulness, breathing exercises, and reflection, helping them manage stress, calm anxiety, and navigate difficulties with emotional strength.

Stress Management

This class explores Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD)—its causes, symptoms, and effects on the brain and emotions. Students will also learn evidence-based strategies for managing stress, including guided imagery exercises and techniques for creating a personal safe space.
