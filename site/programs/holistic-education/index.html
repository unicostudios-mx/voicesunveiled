Programs · Mental health & academics

Holistic and integrative education.

Every woman and girl in Afghanistan deserves not just education, but the emotional strength, healing, and community support to truly thrive. Our approach is trauma-informed and culturally sensitive, helping students feel understood, not judged.

Mental health support

Healing, connecting, and reclaiming self-worth.

Education is not just about acquiring knowledge; it is about healing, connecting, and reclaiming self-worth and creative expression. Many women and girls in Afghanistan continue to endure extreme isolation, violence, uncertainty, and trauma due to Taliban restrictions and gender apartheid. Our mental health support meets this reality with empathy, purpose, and deep respect for each individual's unique path forward.

Professional therapy is complemented by regular one-on-one support sessions for academic, familial, and professional goals.

Academic support

Breaking through barriers to achievement.

We empower students to break through barriers to academic achievement, establish clear objectives, and maintain their drive to succeed. Beyond our courses, we connect students with broader educational pathways and ensure they receive comprehensive support for lasting success, including internet funding so they can reliably join their classes from anywhere.

Free test-prep guides, scholarship eligibility guides, an English learning database, and a college-essay program round out the academic resources available to every student.

Elective courses

Skills, creativity, and opportunity.

In addition to our core empowerment curriculum, Voices Unveiled offers a variety of elective courses taught by dedicated volunteer instructors from around the world, designed to develop skills, inspire creativity, and expand opportunities according to student interest and demand.

Functional skills

English Language (Levels 1, 2, and 3)

Structured lessons and test preparation for TOEFL and Duolingo, supporting students to improve their skills and access global education and career opportunities.

Data Analytics

An introduction to analyzing and interpreting data using simple tools and real-life examples, preparing students for modern job markets.

Computer Programming

Foundational programming skills using beginner-friendly languages, with hands-on practice and mentorship from international tech volunteers.

Humanities

Philosophy and Physics

A unique opportunity to explore deep questions about existence, logic, and the universe, learning scientific theories and sparking curiosity and critical thinking.

Creative Writing

Explores self-discovery and expression through journaling, storytelling, and poetry while strengthening creativity, writing skills, and community connection.

The Life of Ideas

A journey into how we think, why we believe what we do, and how ideas shape the world and us. Students explore the big patterns of thought that run through philosophy and everyday life.

Art-Making: What Lives Within

A journey through the inner world through artistic expression, using drawing and other art forms to transform thoughts and emotions into visual stories that offer a path to healing, self-discovery, and empowerment.

Sciences

Medical Science

Led by a practicing physician, this class explores anatomy, neuroscience, cell biology, infectious disease, and more.

To the Stars and Beyond

Explore the wonders of astronomy, from the Big Bang to the galaxies above us. This class celebrates discovery, curiosity, and the contributions of women in science.

Bio Science and Sustainability

Explore the exciting world of biology and ecology to better understand the world around us and create a more sustainable future.

Wellness

Meditation

A supportive space for students to build inner peace and resilience through mindfulness, breathing exercises, and reflection, helping them manage stress, calm anxiety, and navigate difficulties with emotional strength.

Stress Management

Explores post-traumatic stress, its causes, symptoms, and effects on the brain and emotions, with evidence-based strategies including guided imagery and techniques for creating a personal safe space.
