Programs · Guest Speakers

Distinguished Guest Speakers

At Voices Unveiled, our guest speakers bring stories, wisdom, and inspiration that leave a lasting impact on our students. From global leaders to entrepreneurs, practitioners, activists and artists, each opens doors to new ideas, sparks critical thinking, and helps cultivate a dynamic, energizing space for shared discovery and creativity.

Through these interactive sessions, students don’t just listen—they are moved, challenged, and inspired to dream bigger, act boldly, and see themselves as part of a larger story of hope and change.

Here are just some of the remarkable guests who have shared their insights with students in our Empowerment Course:

Dr. Lyla June Johnson

Indigenous musician, Scholar, Scientist, Orator, and Community organizer of Diné (Navajo), Tsétsêhéstâhese (Cheyenne) and European lineages.

Zainab Salbi

Honoree of the Time100 Impact Award, named one of the “25 Women Changing the World” by People Magazine, Founder of Women for Women International, Humanitarian and Co-founder of Daughters for Earth, Best selling author (Freedom is an Inside Job), TV host (#MeToo, Now What?) and inspirational speaker.

“ I am so touched by the work Voices Unveiled does. Everyone should do anything they can to support them. ”

We are always seeking passionate speakers who are ready to ignite curiosity, broaden perspectives, and share their experiences. Whether you are an innovator, advocate, or storyteller, your voice has the power to inspire the next generation of leaders.

If you would like to volunteer an hour of your time, we would love to hear from you. Please get in touch at hello@voicesunveiled.org
