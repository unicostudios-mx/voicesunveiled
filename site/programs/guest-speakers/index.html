Distinguished guest speakers.

Our guest speakers bring stories, wisdom, and inspiration that leave a lasting impact on our students. From global leaders to entrepreneurs, practitioners, activists, and artists, each opens doors to new ideas and sparks critical thinking.

Moved, challenged, and inspired to dream bigger.

Through these interactive sessions, students don't just listen. They are moved, challenged, and inspired to dream bigger, act boldly, and see themselves as part of a larger story of hope and change. Here are just some of the remarkable guests who have shared their insights with students in our Empowerment Course.

Dr. Lyla June Johnson

Indigenous musician, scholar, scientist, orator, and community organizer of Diné (Navajo), Tsétsêhéstâhese (Cheyenne) and European lineages.

Zainab Salbi

Honoree of the Time100 Impact Award, named one of the "25 Women Changing the World" by People Magazine, founder of Women for Women International, humanitarian, co-founder of Daughters for Earth, best-selling author, TV host, and inspirational speaker.

Share an hour of your time.

We are always seeking passionate speakers ready to ignite curiosity and share their experiences. Whether you are an innovator, advocate, or storyteller, your voice can inspire the next generation of leaders.
