One-line: An urgent-campaign banner — warm but serious (midnight ground, gold accents, never red), with an optional progress bar toward a goal.

```jsx
<CampaignBanner
  title="Help keep Voices Unveiled open."
  body="We must raise $50,000 to continue education and mental health support."
  raised={32000} goal={50000}
/>
```

Pass `raised` + `goal` to render the progress bar. Keep tone urgent, not
alarmist. Use sparingly — one active campaign at a time.
