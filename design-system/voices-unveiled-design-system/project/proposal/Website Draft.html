About us

A grassroots lifeline for Afghan women and girls.

Voices Unveiled is a donor-powered nonprofit opening pathways to education, healing, and leadership — even where the world's doors have closed.

Photograph — students learning

From our Executive Director

"You are empowering a new generation."

With the withdrawal of U.S. AID, economic and educational opportunities in Afghanistan have diminished further — making Voices Unveiled one of the few remaining pathways to education and empowerment for Afghan women.

Together, we can ensure Afghan women and girls have access to education, opportunity, and hope.

Cara Cruickshank
Executive Director

Programs

Education, healing, and leadership.

Free, online, and built around the whole person — from the classroom to mental health to career.

Education

Self-Empowerment Course

Two full semesters: mental health and self-care, women's health, leadership, critical thinking, and human rights.

Mental health

Trauma-Informed Care

Professional therapy for students plus regular one-on-one support for academic, familial, and professional goals.

Leadership

Mentorship

English language and proficiency exam prep, data analytics, computer programming, and philosophy of physics.

Access

Internet Funding

Internet packages so students can reliably join their classes from anywhere.

Resources

Academic Resources

Free test-prep guides, scholarship eligibility guides, an English learning database, and a college-essay program.

Community

Guest Speakers & Arts

Renowned speakers and sustained women's arts programs that build confidence and connection.

Questions

Giving with confidence.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Yes. Voices Unveiled is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit; your gift is tax-deductible to the full extent allowed by law.

Where does my money go?

Directly to scholarships, internet access, course materials, mental health support, and emergency assistance for Afghan women and girls.

Can I give monthly?

Yes — our Monthly Donors Circle accepts a recurring gift of any amount, providing the steady support our students rely on.
