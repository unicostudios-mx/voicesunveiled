The opportunity

One of the few remaining lifelines.

In 2024 you provided 115 scholarships, funded internet for 50 students, and trained 20 interns — even as the withdrawal of U.S. AID narrowed the paths to education for Afghan women and girls.

The new website should carry that weight with clarity and calm — organized around a single promise, keep her learning, where every page makes the next step obvious. It builds directly on the Voice & Dignity design system.

Voices Unveiled 02

What the redesign should achieve

Six goals.

1

Make giving effortless

An always-visible Donate path and an impact-based selector that shows what each gift makes possible.

2

Lead with dignity

Center student agency and resilience — never trauma as spectacle — with consent-first storytelling.

3

Build trust

Surface real impact numbers, the annual report, partners, and 501(c)(3) transparency near every ask.

4

Feel calm, not crowded

Generous space and a focused structure that works on any device and connection speed.

5

Be safe & accessible

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard focus, descriptive alt text, and student-safety practices built in.

6

Grow with you

A reusable component library so new pages and campaigns stay on-brand without redesign.

Voices Unveiled 04

How the key pages work

Page by page.

Homepage

  • Hero: "Keep her learning" + Sponsor a Student
  • Short mission statement & 2024 impact numbers
  • Student story preview & programs overview
  • Testimonial, donation block, volunteer CTA

Donation

  • Campaign message (urgent or evergreen)
  • Impact-by-amount selector + monthly toggle
  • "Where your money goes" + 501(c)(3) tax info
  • Short student testimonial & FAQ

Story

  • Story introduction + safety note
  • Student quote & personal journey
  • Program impact
  • CTA: Sponsor a student like her

Impact

  • Impact headline + key numbers
  • Program outcomes & testimonials
  • Downloadable annual report
  • Financial transparency + CTA to donate
