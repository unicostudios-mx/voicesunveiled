{{ eyebrow('The invitation') }}

The Reclamation is a live, intimate workshop for women who are done pretending or playing small.

It is for women ready to deepen the work of reclaiming their inner voice, their boundaries, and their wholeness — as both an act of self-care and sovereignty. Developed through the transformative work of Voices Unveiled with Afghan women leaders, these practices are now open to women across Europe, the Americas, and beyond.

This is not another self-improvement program. It is not about optimizing yourself, fixing yourself, or becoming a better version of yourself. It is about recovering the relationship with yourself that existed before performance became survival.

{{ eyebrow('A different story about women and power') }}

Before the story we inherited, there were other stories.

Across some of the world's oldest living cultures, women have long held a different kind of authority. Not power over others, but power as the center — as origin point, the quiet, life-giving force from which everything else emerges and around which community organizes.

Women, in this worldview, are not the support system. They are the system — the center from which relationships, care, continuity, and collective wellbeing radiate. The Reclamation is an invitation to return to that organizing principle: one that places your wholeness not at the margins, but at the center, where it has always belonged.

The natural world is our first teacher — an ecosystem thrives through reciprocity, interdependence, and living beings tending to one another.
{{ eyebrow('Reclaim your voice · unlearn the conditioning', gold=true) }}

You know the feeling.

For many women, living through this moment creates a suffering that often goes unnamed — not trauma in the clinical sense, but a relentless accumulation of vigilance, grief, rage, and overwhelm. These are not personal failures. They are symptoms of a culture that asks more of women while allowing them less room to exist fully.

  • The chronic self-monitoring and compulsive caregiving.
  • Burnout mistaken for virtue; achievement that costs you your own aliveness.
  • Answering emails at midnight while apologizing for having boundaries.
  • Shrinking in rooms you have every right to occupy.
  • The fatigue that sleep cannot repair — because it comes from staying slightly removed from yourself in order to remain “acceptable” inside your own life.
  • Success at the cost of becoming someone you no longer quite recognize.

The Reclamation is a space to examine how these patterns become internalized, loosen their grip, and return to a deeper relationship with yourself — through feminist literature, artistic reflection, meaningful conversation, and shared practice.

{{ eyebrow('Why this workshop is effective') }}

The results are documented. They are not anecdotal.

Three years ago, Afghan refugees asked Cara Cruickshank to design a feminist self-empowerment course for women still living under Taliban rule. She said yes — and founded Voices Unveiled, an underground online school operating in secret. Among her students: graduate students, doctors, lawyers, activists, artists, and engineers risking everything to keep learning.

{{ eyebrow('About Cara Cruickshank') }}

Two decades creating spaces for women to reclaim their voices.

Cara Cruickshank is a feminist educator, writer, artistic director, producer, and human rights advocate. Her journey began in Bahia, Brazil, teaching self-empowerment to marginalized girls who walked barefoot across the hills to attend her classes. From there she guided high-school girls, taught gender studies, and led workshops for junior-high boys on deconstructing masculinity.

She has led international theater projects across Europe and the US, produced a critically acclaimed play about the female experience that premiered in Paris at the height of the #MeToo movement, and spent more than a decade curating Café de la Culture — a global series of philosophical art salons. When the racial-justice uprisings of 2020 demanded urgent dialogue, she moderated live panels with frontline activists around the world.

When Afghan refugees approached her to build a feminist course for women under Taliban rule, she did not hesitate. The result — Voices Unveiled — has since reached hundreds of Afghan women across eight provinces. Now, for the first time, Cara brings this same methodology to a global cohort through The Reclamation.
