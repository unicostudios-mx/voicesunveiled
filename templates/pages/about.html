A grassroots lifeline for Afghan women and girls.

Voices Unveiled is a donor-powered nonprofit opening pathways to education, healing, and leadership — even where the world's doors have closed.

{{ eyebrow('Our mission') }}

Together, we shape brighter futures.

Voices Unveiled launched in February 2023, answering a desperate call from Afghan refugee women in the wake of the Taliban's resurgence after the U.S. troop withdrawal. Their plea was simple: provide free online education in self-empowerment and feminism to Afghan women and girls sidelined from society.

Our mission expanded swiftly, reaching beyond borders to empower women in Afghanistan, bolstering mental health and fostering connection in the face of adversity. Voices Unveiled empowers Afghan women with free online education, mental health support, leadership development, and community building.

We envision a world where the silenced are heard, where empathy fuels action, and where compassionate leaders rise to drive change. We are committed to building a global community founded on justice, equality, and the profound impact of self-expression, education, and empowerment.

Cara Cruickshank, Founder and Program Director

{{ eyebrow('Our founder') }}

“You are empowering a new generation.”

Cara Cruickshank is an educator, community organizer, and women's advocate who has lived in France, Brazil, and the U.S. She is founder and host of Cafe de la Culture, a nonprofit that facilitates cultural exchange and intersectional community conversations, and writer, director, and producer of theater for social change, including On the Subject of Girls, The Alchemy of Imperfection, and The Halloween Journey.

Cara has designed and led empowerment, diversity, and gender studies workshops for marginalized and international communities in the U.S., Brazil, Turkey, and France, including programs through LaGuardia Performing Arts Center, New York's Board of Cooperative Educational Services, and the University of Balikesir. She has curated and moderated panels with frontline activists, indigenous leaders, scholars, and artists on topics such as #MeToo, Black Lives Matter, Indigenous rights, and the refugee crisis.

In 2022 she co-designed and ran Global Village Day Camp, an empowerment and holistic education summer camp for refugee and immigrant youth in Aurora, Colorado, spoke on women's advocacy at Bard College and the University of Michigan, and was Featured Poet at Spoken Word Paris. In February 2023, Cara launched her Self-Empowerment and Feminism course for Afghan women with the Afghan Youth Leaders Assembly and later partnered with Generations of Change.

With the withdrawal of U.S. aid, economic and educational opportunities in Afghanistan have diminished further, making Voices Unveiled one of the few remaining pathways to education and empowerment for Afghan women. Together, we can ensure they have access to education, opportunity, and hope.
