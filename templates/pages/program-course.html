A lifeline where education is banned.

In a country where education is banned, self-expression is forbidden, self-empowerment is discouraged, and feminism is illegal, our course offers a lifeline. Students risk their lives to attend our underground online sessions, which meet three times a week. They seek to learn about their rights, to express themselves freely, and to have their existence acknowledged.

Our course provides a comprehensive exploration of mental health practices, aiming to build self-compassion, self-esteem, self-awareness, and self-care. Each trimester features a diverse range of holistic topics, including creative writing, discourse, guest speakers, health and nutrition, mindfulness, and women's studies.

The course extends across Afghanistan, reaching students aged 15 to 40 and offering them a vital connection to their own sense of humanity and relevance. Many have expressed that, since the U.S. withdrawal and the Taliban's rise to power, this program has been a crucial lifeline, making them feel truly seen and heard for the first time in years.
