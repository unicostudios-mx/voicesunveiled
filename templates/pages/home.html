{{ eyebrow('Why now') }}

Since 2021, Afghan women have faced gender apartheid.

Women in Afghanistan are banned from school, work, and most of public life. Discussions about emotions, health, and rights are forbidden, and mental health support is inaccessible. Many are grappling with poverty and isolation.

Voices Unveiled is here to provide online education, empowerment, and mental health support to women in Afghanistan and neighboring countries. With the withdrawal of U.S. aid, we are one of the few remaining pathways to education for them.
