{{ eyebrow('Mental health support') }}

Healing, connecting, and reclaiming self-worth.

Education is not just about acquiring knowledge; it is about healing, connecting, and reclaiming self-worth and creative expression. Many women and girls in Afghanistan continue to endure extreme isolation, violence, uncertainty, and trauma due to Taliban restrictions and gender apartheid. Our mental health support meets this reality with empathy, purpose, and deep respect for each individual's unique path forward.

Professional therapy is complemented by regular one-on-one support sessions for academic, familial, and professional goals.

{{ eyebrow('Academic support') }}

Breaking through barriers to achievement.

We empower students to break through barriers to academic achievement, establish clear objectives, and maintain their drive to succeed. Beyond our courses, we connect students with broader educational pathways and ensure they receive comprehensive support for lasting success, including internet funding so they can reliably join their classes from anywhere.

Free test-prep guides, scholarship eligibility guides, an English learning database, and a college-essay program round out the academic resources available to every student.
