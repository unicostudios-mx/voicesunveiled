Surviving, and thriving, despite hardship.

Amid the ongoing crisis, many families are unable to provide adequate nourishment, leading to preventable health issues among our young women, including scurvy, rickets, and anemia. These conditions cause severe symptoms such as bleeding gums, bone and joint pain, chronic headaches, dizziness, fatigue, and profound depression.

Such health challenges make it incredibly difficult for students to focus on their education and achieve their goals. At Voices Unveiled, we take a holistic approach to support our students' success on every level. By contributing, you help ensure that our Afghan students not only survive but also thrive.
